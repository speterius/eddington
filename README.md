# Eddington experiment

Plans to reproduce the 1919 Eddington experiment: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddington_experiment

Let's see if Einstein's GR prediction stands in the real world!
This repo captures plans, schematics, bills of materials, experiment design, simulation and other documents related to the experiment where we will travel to the 2027 August total solar eclipse, take photos of the stars near the Sun, compare them to their normal location and measure the gravitational deflection.

For every arcsecond that Newtonian physics predicts, gravitational lensing adds another arcsecond to it.
